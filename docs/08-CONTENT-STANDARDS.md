# Kolmari Content Standards

## Voice and tone

- Direct, practical, informed, confident, human
- No aspirational filler ("embark on your journey", "unlock your potential")
- No generic travel-app language
- Sentence case in product UI
- Uppercase with tracking only in compact labels

## Hierarchy rules

- Every page answers: Where am I? / What does this mean for me? / What should I do next?
- Primary action is always visible — do not hide behind accordions
- Secondary detail uses progressive disclosure
- Empty states explain what will appear when data is available

## Provenance labeling (Greenbook)

Every Greenbook entry must be labeled as one of:
- Editorial context
- Verified resource
- Community-reported experience

Never mix these categories without labels.

## Fabrication prohibition

Do not write copy that implies:
- A user qualifies for a visa without profile completion
- A specific Match Score when profile is not complete
- Verified community stories when they are editorial prompts
- Statistics that are not verified

## Empty states

Every page that shows user data must have an explicit empty state:
- Dashboard: show "Start your plan" prompts
- Destinations: "Explore Your World to find destinations"
- My Plan: "Start your plan to track your relocation"
- Kolmari Klub: "Community features are in development"
- Documents: "Upload your first document"
